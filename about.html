The Smart Library was established in 2025 with the goal of improving access to knowledge through modern digital and physical resources. It was designed to support students, teachers, and researchers by making learning easier, faster, and more organized. The main purpose of this library is to promote education, encourage self-learning, and provide quick access to a wide range of books and study materials. It helps users save time by offering a smart system for searching, exploring, and managing books efficiently.

The library offers books in the following categories. These books are available to everyone

  • Programming
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • History
  • Science
  • Literature
